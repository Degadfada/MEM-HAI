# Authors and credits

## Developer

**Edimekabasi E. AKpaneka** — design, direction and development.

The shape of this product came from a single requirement, stated plainly: the system should sit as the
**AI–human handshake** across every project, and be the source of truth for what was done, why it was
done, who gave the go-ahead, and when. Every design decision here traces back to that sentence,
including the one that mattered most — that a record which is complete for one AI vendor and blank for
the rest is not a source of truth, so provenance had to be vendor-neutral from the ground up.

## Upstream

This product began from an MIT-licensed ledger prototype that contributed the original hash-chained
event log, the secret redactor, the budgeted brief distiller, the guardrail and task-contract model,
and the first MCP server. That prototype is preserved as the first commit in this repository, so every
change since reads as a diff against it.
